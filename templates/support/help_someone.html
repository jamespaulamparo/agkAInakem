Supporting Someone Else

Four grounded, practical steps to check in on a friend or loved one.

1

Notice Changes

Look for persistent shifts: withdrawing from conversations, looking perpetually exhausted, expressing hopelessness, or uncharacteristic silence.

2

Ask Gently

Choose a quiet, unhurried moment. Use open-ended, caring phrasing:

“I've noticed you seem carrying a lot lately, and I just wanted to check in. How are you really doing?”
3

Listen Without Fixing

Your role is not to provide immediate medical solutions. Acknowledge that their struggle is real. Avoid dismissing their pain with 'It could be worse.'

4

Connect to Care

Offer to accompany them or help connect with ITRMC Psychiatry in Parian, a counselor, or the NCMH hotline (1553).

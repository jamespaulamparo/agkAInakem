Helping Someone Who May Be Struggling

When you notice changes in a friend, classmate, or coworker, knowing how to approach them can feel daunting. The Notice → Ask → Listen → Connect framework provides simple, grounded guidance.

1

Notice Changes

Look for subtle shifts in behavior over time rather than a single off-day: withdrawing from conversations, looking perpetually exhausted, expressing hopelessness, or sudden uncharacteristic irritability.

2

Ask Gently

Choose a private, relaxed moment. Use open-ended, non-judgmental language:

"I've noticed you seem carrying a lot lately, and I just wanted to check in. How are things really going?"
3

Listen Without Fixing

Your job is not to diagnose or provide immediate solutions. Give them space to speak, validate their feelings ("That sounds really exhausting"), and avoid dismissing their experience with "it could be worse."

4

Connect to Support

Encourage them to take a manageable step toward support: speaking to a counselor, contacting a designated responder in their school/organization, or calling a trusted service. Offer to sit with them or help them reach out.

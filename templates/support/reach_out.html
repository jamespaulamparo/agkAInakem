Non-Blocking First Aid

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Practice (Panangikabil ti Bagi iti Agdama)

When anxiety or overwhelm (pannakariro wenno panagdanag) spikes, this simple sensory exercise helps bring your awareness back to the safe present moment. You can skip this anytime if you need immediate human help.

5 Things you see

Notice five objects around you.

4 Things you touch

Feel your feet, your desk, or cloth.

3 Things you hear

Listen for wind, traffic, or a clock.

2 Things you smell

Notice ambient scents in the room.

1 Thing you taste

Take a sip of water or notice your breath.

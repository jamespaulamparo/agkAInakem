Transparency & Ethics

About Words For It

Words For It is a browser-based mental health literacy and early-support platform. We help people discover language for emotional experiences, understand mental health concepts, and connect with trustworthy human support.

1. What Words For It Is — and Is Not

What We Do

  • Provide a curated, reviewed dictionary of mental health vocabulary.
  • Help you find words for complex feelings based on phrases you enter.
  • Explain clear differences between similar concepts (e.g. stress vs. burnout).
  • Offer step-by-step educational first-aid and grounding practices.
  • Guide friends, family, and peers on how to notice, ask, listen, and connect.
  • Facilitate contact with verified school, community, or workplace responders.

What We Explicitly Do Not Do

  • We do not diagnose: We never generate diagnostic labels or assess whether someone is clinically ill.
  • We do not replace professionals: Our educational tools do not substitute for clinical therapy, counseling, or psychiatric treatment.
  • We do not simulate a therapist: There is no conversational AI acting as a mental health counselor.
  • We do not score risk or monitor you: We do not secretly calculate risk scores or track your internet activity.

2. Privacy by Default

Mental health information is deeply personal and sensitive. Words For It is engineered with strong privacy protections:

  • Anonymous browsing: Browsing the dictionary, reading distinctions, and exploring sentences works completely without an account.
  • No text retention by default: Submitted discovery sentences are processed in-memory to match curated concepts and are not retained or sold to third parties.
  • Privacy-preserving check-ins: Any optional self-reported check-in statuses are decoupled from personal identifiers and never exposed to organizational administrators.

3. Companion Browser Extension

The Words For It Chromium companion extension brings the curated dictionary to your web browsing:

  • Contextual highlight lookup: Select a term or phrase on any webpage and right-click to view the verified definition and distinction popup.
  • Zero background tracking: The extension does not monitor browsing history, inspect webpages silently, or track user activity. It only reads text you explicitly choose to look up.
  • Shared database: Lookups query the exact same reviewed mental health database as the website.

4. Editorial & Curated Relationships

Unlike a generic dictionary or an uncontrolled generative AI model, every mental health concept in Words For It is organized with explicit relationship types (such as Similar to, Different from, Can contribute to, and Often confused with). Definitions cite recognized sources (such as WHO ICD-11, APA, or verified psychological literature) to ensure trustworthiness.

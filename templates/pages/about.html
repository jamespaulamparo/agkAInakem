About & Principles

Core safety boundaries, privacy guarantees, and our mission in San Fernando, La Union.

1. Non-Diagnostic Educational Scope

agkAInakem provides vocabulary to help people articulate their experiences. We explicitly do not diagnose, prescribe, predict clinical risk, or simulate an artificial therapist.

2. Strict Privacy by Default

Exploring vocabulary and checking in is completely anonymous. No account is required. We do not track browsing history, sell data, or profile users.

3. Grounded in San Fernando, La Union

Instead of unreachable overseas hotlines, we link directly to Ilocos Training and Regional Medical Center (ITRMC) Psychiatry in Parian, San Fernando CDRRMO (911), and the Philippine National Crisis Hotline (1553).
